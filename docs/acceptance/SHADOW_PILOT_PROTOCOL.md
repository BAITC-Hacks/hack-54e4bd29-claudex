# Shadow pilot protocol — gate draft frozen 2026-09-23

**Not started. Owner approval required.** This protocol records external decisions and evidence requirements; it is not permission to enable operational model alerts or conduct a clinical trial.

Before observation, the process owner must identify participating mapped organizations, coordinator and reviewers, human action workflow, eligible roles, observation period, response procedure, alert budget, incident escalation, and withdrawal/rollback authority. Infrastructure owner must select RPO/RTO and backup retention. Names, dates and thresholds remain **UNKNOWN / EXTERNAL DEPENDENCY** until documented approval; do not backfill them after viewing outcomes.

Entry requires completed D delivery/mapping gates, frozen M2 policy and evaluation provenance, applicable R1/R2 scope/workflow evidence and R3/R4 technical controls. POLICY_NOT_APPROVED, INSUFFICIENT_DATA or STALE results keep operational model alerts closed. An isolated realm, historical replay, synthetic fixture, generated model report or developer benchmark cannot replace those gates.

For each approved observation window, reviewers use authorized aggregates only and record delivery completeness/freshness, canonical scope/mapping version, policy/model/artifact versions, explanation usefulness, review/response time, false alerts and missed episodes using the frozen episode definition. Report denominators, exclusions and uncertainty; compare with the frozen policy and baseline workflow. Avoid patient values, source identifiers, tokens and credentials in reports. Forecast growth is not an instruction to redistribute patients. Humans review alerts; no automatic medical or management action is allowed.

Predefine missing-delivery, stale-model, revoked-mapping, scope-leak and excessive-alert stopping conditions. If episode count or independent observation duration is insufficient, report INSUFFICIENT_DATA rather than successful accuracy. Technical failures retain FAIL/NOT TESTED statuses separately from external dependencies.

Rollback disables new model alerts and retains descriptive analytics, audit/history and all failed forecasts. Do not delete unfavorable observations or change test thresholds to improve the report. The owner signs a dated decision with limited scope, accepted evidence, unresolved risks and rollback authority only after review. Until a real signature exists, the report remains NOT ADMITTED.
